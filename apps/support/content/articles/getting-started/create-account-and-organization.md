Preprocess accounts use passwordless sign-in. You don't set or manage a
password—sign up with an emailed sign-in link or Google, then create your first
organization.

## Sign up on Preprocess Cloud

1. Open the Preprocess sign-up page.
2. Enter your work email address and select **Sign up**, or select **Continue
   with Google**.
3. If you used email, open the sign-in link in your inbox. Each link works once
   and expires after 15 minutes.
4. Enter your name when prompted.
5. Create your organization by entering an organization name.

Your first organization starts on the Free plan. You're assigned the **Owner**
role automatically, which lets you invite teammates, configure billing and
manage organization settings.

## Join an existing organization

If a teammate invited you to an organization, open the invitation link in your
email. Sign in with the same email address the invitation was sent to (or with
Google if it uses that address), then accept the invitation.

On self-hosted installs, open sign-up is disabled—new members join by invitation
from an Owner or Admin.

## Rename your organization or find its ID

Open **Settings → Organization → General** to rename your organization or copy
your **Organization ID** (needed when configuring the CLI or API calls).
