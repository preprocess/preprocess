You can reach **Preprocess Support** by email or from inside the app. Every
request is answered first by the Preprocess Support agent and handed off to a
person whenever needed.

## How to contact support

- **Email:** send a message to `support@preprocess.com` from the email address
  on your Preprocess account.
- **In the app:** open **Help → Contact support** in the bottom-left sidebar.
- **Team and Enterprise:** your organization can also use a shared **Slack
  Connect** channel once enabled with Preprocess Support.

An interactive web form, in-app support side sheet and **My requests** view are
coming soon to the help center. Until they launch, email
`support@preprocess.com` directly and include your **Organization ID** (found in
**Settings → Organization → General**) plus any relevant agent slug, deployment
ID or case ID.

## How Preprocess Support works

Every support request runs as a case worked first by our support agent:

1. **Automated triage:** the agent checks the help center, docs and read-only
   context from your organization (such as your plan, usage and deploy status)
   to give an immediate answer. Every read-only access to your organization is
   recorded in your
   [audit log](/security-and-sso/data-privacy-retention-and-audit-logs).
2. **Human handoff:** Severity 1 incidents, billing adjustments, refunds,
   data-deletion requests, low-confidence answers or any request where you ask
   for a person go straight to our team. All replies come from **Preprocess
   Support**.

## First-response targets (SLAs)

First-response targets are measured in **business hours (Pacific Time)** unless
noted. The support agent's initial reply doesn't count toward the SLA unless it
solves your request.

| Plan           | Channels                                       | Sev 1       | Sev 2          | Sev 3           |
| -------------- | ---------------------------------------------- | ----------- | -------------- | --------------- |
| **Free**       | Form, email                                    | Best effort | Best effort    | Best effort     |
| **Team**       | Form, email, API, Slack Connect                | 8h          | 1 business day | 2 business days |
| **Enterprise** | Form, email, API, Slack Connect, named contact | 1h, 24/7    | 4h             | 1 business day  |

### Severity guidelines

- **Sev 1:** production agents are down or unable to process cases.
- **Sev 2:** a major feature is degraded with a workaround available.
- **Sev 3:** general questions, configuration help or minor issues.

## Self-hosted installs

If you run a self-hosted install with an active license, email
`support@preprocess.com` with your release tag and error logs. Self-hosted
installs never send support data to Preprocess automatically. See
[Configure support links on a self-hosted install](/self-hosting/configure-support-links-for-self-hosting).
