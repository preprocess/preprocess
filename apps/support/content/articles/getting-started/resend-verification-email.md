Preprocess doesn't use passwords or a separate account-verification email.
Opening your emailed sign-in link verifies your address and signs you in at the
same time.

## Resend a sign-in link

Each sign-in link works once and expires after **15 minutes**. To send a new
one:

1. On the **Check your email** screen, select **send another** at the bottom—or
   return to the sign-in page.
2. Enter your email address again and select **Sign in** (or **Sign up**).
3. Open the newest email and click the link once.

Requesting a new link doesn't lock your account, but only use the most recent
link you received.

## If the email doesn't arrive or says it expired

- **Check spam and junk folders** for messages from Preprocess.
- **Watch for corporate link scanners.** Some email security gateways pre-fetch
  links in incoming messages, which consumes a one-time sign-in link before you
  click it. If every link immediately shows an expired error, ask your IT team
  to allowlist Preprocess sign-in emails or sign in with **Continue with
  Google** (or SSO on Enterprise).
- **Check your email address.** Select **Use a different email** on the
  confirmation screen if you mistyped the address.

## Resend a team invitation

Invitation links are separate from sign-in links. If you didn't receive an
invite to an organization, ask an **Owner** or **Admin** to open **Settings →
Organization → Members**, check your pending invitation and re-invite you.
