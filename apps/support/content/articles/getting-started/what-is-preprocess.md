---
title: What is Preprocess
description:
  Preprocess deploys and runs AI agents that work cases for your team.
updated: 2026-11-28
---

Preprocess runs AI agents for your organization. Each request an agent works is
a case, and you can follow every case in the app.

To learn how agents, cases and deployments fit together, read the
[docs](https://docs.preprocess.com).
