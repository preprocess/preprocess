Organization **Owners** manage payment details and invoices in **Settings →
Organization → Billing** under **Payment**.

## Add or update your card

1. Open **Settings → Organization → Billing**.
2. Scroll to **Payment**.
3. Next to **Payment method**, select **Update** (or **Add a card** if no card
   is on file yet).
4. Enter your new card details and save.

Preprocess doesn't store raw card numbers—the button takes you to our secure
payment portal and returns you to Billing when finished.

## Change your billing email

Invoices and payment receipts go to your organization's billing email.

1. Open **Settings → Organization → Billing**.
2. Under **Payment**, select **Edit** next to **Billing email**.
3. Enter the new email address (for example your accounts-payable alias) and
   save.

## View and download invoices

1. Open **Settings → Organization → Billing**.
2. Under **Payment**, find your three most recent invoices and select **View
   invoice** on any row to open or download it.
3. Select **All invoices** to browse and download your full invoice history.

## If a payment fails (past due)

When a recurring charge fails, your account enters a **7-day grace period** and
displays a **Last payment failed** banner in Billing (plus a **Payment failed**
notice in the sidebar for Owners and Admins).

Select **Update card** from the banner—or **Update** under **Payment**—to add a
working card and bring your account current.

## Enterprise invoicing

Enterprise organizations pay by invoice (Net 30). To update your billing
contact, purchase order number or invoicing terms,
[contact support](/getting-started/contact-support-and-response-times).
