---
title: Self-hosted installs
description: You can run Preprocess in your own Cloudflare account.
updated: 2026-11-28
---

A self-hosted install runs Preprocess in your own Cloudflare account, on your
own Postgres database. Its data stays in your account.

The
[self-hosting guide](https://docs.preprocess.com/deploy-and-operate/self-hosting)
covers the install.
