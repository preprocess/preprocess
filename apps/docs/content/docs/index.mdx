---
title: Preprocess
description: Build, deploy and supervise agents that work cases.
---

An agent on Preprocess is a TypeScript module. It declares its instructions, its
model, the tools it may call, and the shape of the answers it produces for each
case. You deploy it with the CLI. Preprocess runs it on Cloudflare Workers, and
your team supervises its cases in the app: they review answers, approve what
needs approval, and teach it rules it will follow next time.
