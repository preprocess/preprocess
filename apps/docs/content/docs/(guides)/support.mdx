---
title: Support
description: How to get help with Preprocess.
---

For help with Preprocess, email
[support@preprocess.com](mailto:support@preprocess.com). Tell us your
organization and, if it's about a case, its id.

Team and Enterprise customers can ask us for a shared Slack channel. Email
support@preprocess.com from your organization to set one up.

Enterprise support terms are in your contract.
