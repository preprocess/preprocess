---
title: When a license ends
description:
  What a self-hosted install keeps and what turns off when its license key
  expires, and how to renew.
---

A self-hosted install runs the free product without a license. A license key
adds paid features, such as audit log export, SSO, directory sync, synced
groups, custom roles and custom sandbox environments, and sets the install's
seat limit. This page says what happens when that key expires.

**Nothing is deleted when a license ends.** Your cases, agents, deployments,
files, logs and audit log stay in your own Cloudflare account and database, and
the free product keeps working.

## How the key is checked

The license key is a signed file that your install checks offline. There is no
license server, and an install never reports usage or contacts Preprocess, Inc.
to check its license. Keys last one year and are renewed with a new key.

## The 30-day grace period

When the key's expiry date passes, the install enters a 30-day grace period.
Paid features keep working, and Owners and Admins see a warning in the sidebar
and on the **License** settings page with the date grace ends. Members see
nothing.

## After grace ends

When the grace period ends, paid features turn off and the install runs the free
product:

- **Data:** everything stays, and you can still read and export it through the
  API. Audit log export turns off, but the audit log itself keeps recording and
  stays readable in the app and the API.
- **SSO:** people who already sign in with SSO keep signing in. SSO settings
  become read-only.
- **Directory sync** stops. People and groups it created stay as they were.
- **Custom sandbox environments:** agents that use them pause. Their cases don't
  start until the license is renewed, and other agents keep running.
- **Seats:** the license's seat limit no longer applies.

Renewing turns the paid features back on immediately, and paused agents resume.
There is nothing to restore.

## Renewing

Contact us for a new key before your current one expires. To install it, an
Owner pastes it in **Settings → License**, or sets `PREPROCESS_LICENSE_KEY` and
redeploys. A key pasted in Settings takes precedence over the variable. The
change takes effect on the next request, with no restart.
