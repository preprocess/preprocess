---
title: Product analytics and session replay
---

**Product analytics.** We use PostHog, a product analytics service, to
understand how Preprocess is used and to improve it. When you use the Preprocess
app, we record which pages you visit and actions such as signing up, creating an
organization, inviting a teammate, deploying an agent, starting or completing a
case, and changing your plan. These records contain identifiers (such as your
user ID, your organization's ID and the IDs of agents and cases), counts and
fixed categories (such as a plan name or whether a review was approved). They
never contain the content of your cases, documents, prompts or messages, and
they never contain your name or email address. We also record your
organization's plan, seat count and creation date so we can understand usage by
organization.

**Session replay.** To find and fix usability problems, we record how the
Preprocess app's pages are laid out and how you move through them: clicks,
scrolling and page changes. Before a recording leaves your browser, all text on
the page and everything you type are masked. The case room, documents and images
are excluded entirely. Network requests and console logs are not recorded.
Recordings are linked to your user ID and organization and are kept for 30 days.
Self-hosted installations send nothing to Preprocess. An installation sends this
data only to a PostHog project its operator configures.
