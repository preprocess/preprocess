Preprocess Cloud combines active seat pricing, prepaid credits for per-case
usage and monthly storage overage. Self-hosted installs run free without usage
reporting, or unlock paid features with a flat annual seat license.

## Preprocess Cloud plans

Every Preprocess Cloud organization starts a plan at checkout with a payment
card on file (Free included). Plans are managed by the organization's **Owner**.

| Feature              | Free                             | Team                                                   | Enterprise                                             |
| -------------------- | -------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------ | ------------------------------------------------------ |
| **Base fee**         | $0                               | $0                                                     | $50,000/year annual commit                             |
| **Active seats**     | Up to 3 included                 | $16/active seat/month                                  | Draws down annual commit at $16/seat/month             |
| **Cases per month**  | Up to 1,000 included             | Unlimited (first 1,000 free, then tiered case fees)    | Unlimited (first 1,000 free, then tiered case fees)    |
| **Storage included** | 5 GB ($0.10/GB-month overage)    | 50 GB ($0.10/GB-month overage)                         | 50 GB ($0.10/GB-month overage)                         |
| **Starting credits** | $5 one-time credit (500 credits) | Purchased at checkout ($10 minimum)                    | Drawn from annual commit                               |
| **Case sandboxes**   | Presets and `sandbox.packages`   | Presets, `sandbox.packages` and custom `sandbox.setup` | Presets, `sandbox.packages` and custom `sandbox.setup` |
| **Audit log**        | View and verify                  | View and verify                                        | View, verify and export                                |

Enterprise plans are arranged directly with Preprocess; seats, usage and storage
overage draw down the annual commit at Team rates, with usage beyond the commit
billed monthly at those same rates.

## Active seats

Preprocess charges only for **active** developer and operator seats, calculated
when each calendar month closes:

- **Paid seats:** Anyone in the **Developers** group or in any group with
  **operator** access to at least one agent. A person is counted at most once
  per month regardless of how many groups they belong to.
- **Free seats:** **Viewers**, and **Owners or Admins** who are not in the
  Developers group or any operator group.
- **What counts as active:** An operator who worked, approved or commented on a
  case during the month, or a developer who deployed an agent or ran an eval
  suite. Deploys triggered by an organization API key count the person who
  created that key; GitHub App builds count the member who linked the
  repository.

## Prepaid credits and metered usage

On Free and Team, all per-case usage draws from one prepaid **Credits** balance
where **1 credit = $0.01**:

- **Model tokens:** Provider list price plus 5% markup (Kimi K3.1 Pro, DeepSeek
  V4.1 Flash normal and fast, and Qwen 3.8 27B).
- **`decide` calls:** Provider list price plus 5% markup ($0.03675 per million
  input tokens).
- **OCR pages:** Provider list price plus 5% markup ($0.0042 per page).
- **Case sandboxes:** Metered per second awake at 1.5× the underlying container
  rate for the chosen size (`small`, `medium` or `large`). Platform build
  containers and PDF page rendering for OCR are not billed separately.
- **Case fees (Team and Enterprise):** The first 1,000 cases per organization
  each UTC month are free. Beyond 1,000 cases: **$0.02/case** for cases
  1,001–10,000, **$0.01/case** for 10,001–100,000, and **$0.005/case** above
  100,000. Rerunning an existing case does not count as a new case.

### Buying credits and auto-reload

- **Manual top-ups:** Owners on Free or Team can buy credits at any time
  (`POST /v1/orgs/{org}/billing/credits`), with a minimum purchase of **$10**
  (1,000 credits).
- **Auto-reload:** Owners can enable automatic top-ups
  (`PUT /v1/orgs/{org}/billing/auto-reload`) by setting a balance threshold
  (`belowCredits`), a reload amount (`addCredits`, at least $10) and an optional
  `monthlyLimitUsd` cap. If `monthlyLimitUsd` is omitted, it defaults to your
  organization's monthly spending limit.
- **Non-expiring balance:** Prepaid credits never expire. Refunds are handled
  case by case through support.
- **Zero balance:** If your balance reaches zero, new cases pause until you add
  credits. Usage from in-flight cases that crosses zero is recorded as a small
  owed balance and paid first by your next top-up.

### What is billed to your card instead of credits

Active seats ($16/month on Team) and storage overage ($0.10/GB-month past 5 GB
on Free or 50 GB on Team) are billed monthly to the card on file—they do not
draw from prepaid credits. Storage counts uploaded documents, run captures, run
logs and custom sandbox layers; agent code bundles are free.

## Monthly spending limits

Owners and Admins can set an optional **monthly spending limit** in **Settings →
Organization → Guardrails** (or via `PUT /v1/orgs/{org}/spending`). There is no
limit by default.

- **Scope:** Enforces metered usage only—cases, eval runs, standalone OCR calls,
  case sandbox time and case fees across all deployments. Seat charges and
  storage overage are not counted against it.
- **Reset:** Resets automatically on the **1st of each calendar month at 00:00
  UTC**.
- **When reached:** New case creation and metered model, OCR and `decide` calls
  stop with HTTP `402` (`spending_limit_reached`) until the month resets or an
  Owner or Admin raises or removes the limit.

## Moving from Team to Free

Owners can cancel Team and switch to Free at any time
(`POST /v1/orgs/{org}/billing/plan` with `plan: "free"`). Switching takes effect
at the end of the current billing period, and the Owner can cancel the pending
switch (`DELETE /v1/orgs/{org}/billing/plan/change`) any time before then.

Cancelling is never blocked and nothing is deleted. Before confirming,
`GET /v1/orgs/{org}/billing/plan/change/preview` shows the exact reversible
effects based on live usage:

- **More than 3 paid seats:** The Owner picks up to 3 people to keep a developer
  or operator seat (defaulting to Owners first, then the most recently active).
  Everyone else leaves their seat groups and joins the **Viewers** group,
  keeping read access to agents they could see before (including agents deployed
  later). Their previous group memberships are preserved so returning to Team
  lets the Owner restore them with one call
  (`POST /v1/orgs/{org}/billing/roles/restore`).
- **Custom-environment agents:** Agents whose production deployment uses
  `sandbox.setup` pause new cases on Free and resume automatically if you return
  to Team.
- **Storage above 5 GB:** Kept intact and billed to the card at $0.10/GB-month
  rather than deleted or blocked.
- **More than 1,000 cases this month:** New cases wait until the next UTC month.
- **Credits and in-flight work:** Prepaid credits and auto-reload settings carry
  over, and cases already running finish normally.

If you delete an organization entirely, Team ends immediately with a final
invoice to date, no proration refund, and any remaining prepaid credits are
forfeited.

## Self-hosted licensing

Self-hosted installs never meter usage or report telemetry to Preprocess.

- **Without a license key:** A self-hosted install runs the free feature set
  with no organization limit. You pay Cloudflare directly for Workers, Durable
  Objects, R2, Queues, Containers and Workers AI, plus your own Postgres and
  model providers.
- **Commercial license:** Sold as a **flat annual license priced by seat count**
  (developers plus operators) and delivered as an offline Ed25519-signed
  `PREPROCESS_LICENSE_KEY`. The API verifies the signature locally on every
  entitlement read—there is no license server or phone-home call.
- **Soft seat limit:** Admins see a warning when active developer and operator
  seats reach the licensed count. Adding seats past roughly 10% over the
  licensed limit is refused (`402 seat_limit_reached`) until you free a seat or
  extend the license.
- **Expiry and grace period:** After `expiresAt`, a license enters a **30-day
  grace period** with an Admin warning. Once grace ends, paid features turn off
  and the install falls back to the free feature set while your agents, cases
  and data keep working.
